Question 2: University Student Access and Scholarship Verification System
A university is developing a student verification system that determines whether a student is eligible for campus facilities and scholarships. The student enters their department code and semester number. The department codes are 1 for Computer Science, 2 for Software Engineering, and 3 for Information Technology. Each department has different scholarship eligibility criteria.
The system should use a switch statement to identify the department and a nested switch statement to determine the semester-specific requirements. For example, students in Computer Science semesters 1 and 2 require a minimum CGPA of 3.0, while students in semesters 3 and 4 require a minimum CGPA of 3.2. Similar rules apply to other departments.
The student also enters their CGPA, attendance percentage, and whether they have completed the required community service. The program must use logical operators to determine overall scholarship eligibility. A student is eligible only if all required conditions are satisfied. The program should use the conditional operator to display whether the student qualifies for a scholarship or not. Additionally, use the modulus operator to determine whether the semester number is odd or even.
Student Requirements
Write a C program using switch and nested switch statements to determine department-specific scholarship requirements. Use logical operators such as AND and OR for eligibility checking. Use the conditional operator for displaying the final scholarship status and the modulus operator for semester classification.
