Question 1: Smart Campus Cafeteria Billing System
A university cafeteria wants to automate its billing system for students. The cafeteria offers three categories of food: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner. The student first selects a meal category by entering a number from 1 to 3. After selecting the category, the system displays different food options within that category. For example, Breakfast offers Paratha, Omelette, and Tea; Lunch offers Biryani, Burger, and Pizza; Dinner offers Chicken Karahi, BBQ, and Pasta.
The student enters the selected food item number and quantity. The program should calculate the total cost based on the selected category and food item. If the quantity is greater than 5, the student receives a discount of 10 percent. The program should also use the conditional operator to determine whether the discount applies. Finally, the program should display the original bill, discount amount, and final payable amount.
Student Requirements
Write a C program that uses a switch statement for selecting the meal category and a nested switch statement for selecting the food item. Use the conditional operator to calculate the discount eligibility. The program should also use the modulus operator to determine whether the quantity entered is even or odd and display an appropriate message.
